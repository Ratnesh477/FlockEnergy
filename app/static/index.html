Legacy Protocol Discovery & Anomaly Inspector

During our reverse-engineering inspection of the Urja Meter Ops portal, we uncovered internal JSON endpoints, HMAC-SHA256 signature workflows, and 22 records with incomplete hierarchy nodes. Below is the live reconciliation report:

🔐 Authentication & CSRF

• Provider: Better-Auth with session cookie __Secure-better-auth.session_token (TTL: 3600s)
• CSRF Policy: SvelteKit enforces matching Origin and Referer headers on form POSTs.
• Auto-Reauth: Transparent re-authentication on 401 or 302 redirects to prevent client interruption.

⚡ Bulk Export & HMAC

• Bulk Endpoint: /portal/export?page=1 yields all 403 meters in a single call.
• Signature: HMAC-SHA256 of GET\n/portal/export\npage=1\n<timestamp> using key from /portal/keys.
• Headers: x-timestamp and x-signature.

🧬 Data Polymorphism

• Dual Builds: 238 legacy meters vs 165 v2 meters.
• Legacy detail: Array of {parameterName, parameterValue}.
• v2 detail: Stringified JSON inside classData.installed_meter.
• Dates: DD/MM/YYYY HH:mm normalized to ISO 8601 UTC+05:30.
